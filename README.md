# Poke Lab

A phone-friendly Pokemon TCG collection log. Add a card from a photo, match it to its official English catalogue entry (set, set code, collector number, rarity), and track a condition estimate and live prices.

Live site: https://docgonzo420.github.io/PokeLab/

## Where the data comes from

- **Card catalogue:** `cards.json`, built from [PokemonTCG/pokemon-tcg-data](https://github.com/PokemonTCG/pokemon-tcg-data) (English sets through Delta Reign, Nov 2026). Rebuild with `python3 tools/build_index.py <path-to-that-repo> cards.json`. `tools/wrap.py` turns the Claude version of the page into this site's `index.html`.
- **Prices:** TCGplayer and Cardmarket prices from the free [TCGdex](https://tcgdex.dev) API, updated daily (no key), with the [Pokemon TCG API](https://pokemontcg.io) as a fallback. Refreshed when you open the app if older than 6 hours.
- **Finding a card from a photo:** this site reads the collector number (and any card names) off your photo with [Tesseract.js](https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js), which runs in your browser, then matches it to the catalogue. Inside Claude, Claude reads the photo instead.
- **Scanning with the camera:** Add card > Scan with camera opens the live camera. Fit the card in the frame and the collector number is read as you hold it, like a barcode scanner; it needs two matching reads before it picks the card. The frame finds the card by itself: it looks for the card's four edges in the camera picture and moves and resizes to fit, turning yellow when it has one (it needs the same spot twice in a row, so a passing hand doesn't move it). When it finds all four corners it draws a yellow outline on the card itself, turned and tilted to match it, and dims everything around it; the card is then straightened out before the number is read, and the saved photo is the straightened card. Turn off **Find the card and fit the frame to it** to place the frame yourself. Drag the frame to move it and pinch or use the slider to resize it; moving it yourself pauses the card finder for a few seconds, and with the finder off it remembers where you put it. Tap the picture to focus, and use Flash, Camera zoom and Focus where the phone's browser allows them. Pick Continuous to have it read the card as soon as it's in the frame, or Single shot to read it only when you tap Scan card; the app remembers your choice. In Continuous, Scan now reads the frame straight away if the number won't read on its own. The camera view shows the whole picture the camera takes, not a cropped middle, so a card held at a normal distance fits. Needs camera permission and HTTPS.
- **Adding the match:** each match has its own Add button beside it, so one tap saves the right card. After a camera scan, in either mode, the app goes straight back to scanning: put the next card in and it reads it. It won't re-add the card that's still in the frame until you take it away, and Scan again re-reads a card if the match is wrong.
- **Binder page import:** Add card > Binder page takes a photo of a whole page (9, 4, 12 or 16 pockets) and reads the collector number in every pocket. Drag the yellow grid or its corners to fit the pockets; it remembers the grid. Check the matches, change or search any pocket, then Add saves the whole page at once. You can pick several page photos and go through them one by one. Your photo of each pocket is saved with the card.
- **Deck data:** `deck.json` (types, attacks, abilities, regulation marks and what each Trainer does), built from the same repo with `python3 tools/build_deck.py <path-to-that-repo> deck.json`. Standard means regulation marks H, I and J (since the April 2026 rotation); you can change the marks in the Decks tab when the format rotates.
- **Condition:** measure centering by dragging guides over your photo (limits follow PSA's front-centering standards), and pick the overall condition yourself. Inside Claude, Claude also estimates corners, edges and surface. Either way it's an estimate, not a PSA or CGC grade.

## Cards in other languages

Pick **Card language** at the top of Add card (or on the camera screen). The app remembers it, so switch back to English when you're done. The first time you pick a language, its whole card list downloads and is kept in the browser, refreshed every few days. There's no set code to type: photos, camera scans, binder pages and search match across every set of that language.

- **How these cards are read:** Japanese, Korean and Chinese cards print the set code and number small at the bottom left (like `sv8a 010/187`), so the scanner reads that corner larger, in two ways, and offers both results if they differ. A number whose total fits no set counts as a misread rather than a guess.
- **Japanese:** every set on [TCGdex](https://tcgdex.dev) (about 13,000 cards), with pictures. Prices are Cardmarket's, in euros, counted in dollars at the European Central Bank rate from [Frankfurter](https://frankfurter.dev). TCGplayer doesn't price Japanese cards.
- **Traditional Chinese:** TCGdex's own list (Sword & Shield through Scarlet & Violet), with pictures. Sets it hasn't listed yet use the Japanese entry, since these prints share the Japanese set codes and numbers.
- **Korean:** TCGdex has Korean names for only a few sets, so the rest match by set code and number and show the Japanese name.
- **Prices for Korean and Traditional Chinese cards:** no free source prices them, so the card shows the Japanese printing's Cardmarket price for reference, and it isn't counted in your total.
- **Simplified Chinese:** sets have their own codes (like CSV8C) and come from [zsjy947/PTCG-card-data](https://github.com/zsjy947/PTCG-card-data) (CC0, updated weekly), which also names the matching English card. Pictures are the English printing's, and no free source has prices.
- **Another language:** no free catalogue, so you type the name, number and set yourself, with a price of your own if you like. The set name and code stay filled in for the next card.

## Gallery and decks

- **Binders:** New binder (above the filters) makes a named binder, like a trade binder or one per set. Add a card asks which binder to add into, for photos, live scanning and binder pages, and remembers your pick. Opening Add a card while you're looking at one binder adds into that binder. The Binder picker shows all cards, one binder or cards not in a binder, with that binder's value in the header. Move a card between binders from its details. Deleting a binder keeps its cards. Binders are in the backup file and the spreadsheet.
- **Pictures:** show your photo and the catalogue picture side by side (the default) in the binder, gallery and decks, or just one of them.
- **Gallery tab:** your collection as a grid of cards that animate in, tilt toward the pointer, and show value, rarity, quantity and grade.
- **Filters:** search by name, set or number (type `#25` to find a Pokédex number), and filter by set, rarity and type (Grass, Fire and the rest, plus Trainer and Energy).
- **Sorting:** presets for highest or lowest value, recently added, rarest first, best condition, set and number, name A to Z or Z to A, and Pokédex number, plus My order. In the gallery, Arrange cards lets you drag cards into your own order (press and hold first on a phone), or move them one place with the arrows.
- **Select cards:** in the binder or gallery, Select cards lets you tap several cards (or Select all, which takes what your filters show), then move them to a binder, change their finish, copies or condition together, or remove them. Removing asks first and deletes their photos too.
- **Downloads:** the binder and gallery can download the cards they show (all of them, or what your search and filters leave) as a spreadsheet (CSV) or as one picture of the cards in a grid with names, set numbers and prices, in your current sort order, which you can preview, download or share. The picture can use the catalogue images, your own photos (cards without one fall back to the catalogue image) or both side by side. Over 96 cards, the picture is split into several.
- **Decks tab:** build a 60-card deck by hand or around a Pokémon. It checks deck rules and format legality, counts what you already own and what's left to buy, and suggests cards to finish evolution lines, add energy and make the deck more consistent. Suggestions are rule-based, not tournament results. Each suggestion shows the card's price and the sets you can open it in. There's no free source for booster pack prices, so each set links to a TCGplayer search for its packs.

## Storage

On this site your collection, photos and decks stay in this browser (IndexedDB). The Claude copy of the app keeps its own collection in your Claude account; the two don't sync.

**Backup file:** Save backup (under the filters) puts your whole collection, binders, decks and your own photos into one file, `poke-lab-backup-<date>.json`. Restore backup loads that file into any version of the app, on this site, on another device or browser, or in the Claude copy. It only adds what's missing, so loading the same file twice never doubles your cards. The line beside the buttons says when you last saved one and how many cards you've added since. An empty binder also offers Restore a backup file. Keep the file somewhere safe, like iCloud Drive or Google Drive, because clearing the browser's site data deletes the collection. On iPhone, the home-screen app and Safari keep separate collections, so a backup is also how to move cards between them.
